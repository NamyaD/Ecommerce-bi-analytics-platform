# Power BI report specification

1. **Executive Overview:** revenue, orders, AOV, customers, growth, cancellation/return rates; monthly revenue line, revenue by region/category, top products.
2. **Customer Intelligence:** new vs repeat, segment, customer lifetime value proxy, purchase frequency, top-customer contribution.
3. **Product & Seller Intelligence:** product/category ranking, seller ranking, seller cancellation rate, revenue contribution.
4. **Operations:** delivery variance, delayed orders, cancellation, returns and regional operational performance.
5. **Forecasting:** historical revenue/orders, three-month forecast, holdout MAE/RMSE and model limitations.

Use a date slicer and region/category/seller filters consistently. Label the dataset synthetic and identify the forecast as a baseline.
